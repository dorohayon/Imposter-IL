Contact

For any question, problem or request, write to imposteril36@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few days.

Restore purchases

Purchases belong to your store account, not to the device. On a new device, or after reinstalling, sign in with the same Apple or Google account and choose Settings → Restore purchases, or tap a locked category and then Restore purchases.

Cancel Monthly Premium

The subscription renews automatically every month until cancelled. You can cancel at any time, at least 24 hours before the renewal date, and Premium stays active until the end of the paid period. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Imposter: Word Bluff → Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play → profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Imposter: Word Bluff → Cancel subscription.

Refunds

Payment goes through the store, so refund requests go to the store:

  • App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: Request a refund on Google Play

A refunded purchase stops unlocking what it unlocked. Nothing here limits your rights under applicable consumer protection law.

Ads

An active Monthly Premium and Lifetime Premium remove all ads. Buying a single category does not. Where the law requires it, you can change your ad privacy preferences in Settings → Ad privacy preferences.

Report offensive content

You can report a clue from within the game. Clues from a player you reported are hidden on your device from then on. Every report is reviewed within 24 hours, and offensive words the filter missed are added to it. You can also write to us at the address above.

Your data

No account is needed to play. Requests to access, correct or delete data go to the address above, as described in the Privacy Policy.
