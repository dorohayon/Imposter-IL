Imposter: Word Bluff

A social word game in English and Hebrew. Everyone knows the word. Except one.

Every player gets a secret word, except the imposter, who sees only the category. Each player in turn writes a one-word clue, the group votes, and a caught imposter gets one last chance to guess the word. Play online with other players, in a private room with friends, or together on one device.
